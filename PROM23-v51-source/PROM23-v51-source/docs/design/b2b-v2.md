# B2B V2

Company buyers are equally important to private buyers. Catalog and procurement are visible together in the hero; the homepage business section explains selection, supply, invoices and documents. Manufacturing/status/history/awards/export support both audiences.

Primary procurement: attach a specification, estimate, drawing or photo; or describe the need. SKU and organization are not mandatory to ask for a proposal. Name, phone, structured settlement and source consent choices are explicit. Email/company/INN optional. Known-article quick order remains a secondary shortcut. Errors retain the entered data; no invented lead reference or accepted status.

Business page presents source advantages, equipment, project cases and FAQs in mobile sections. Production page uses real company imagery, manufacturing possibilities, timeline, awards, export and relevant contact/tour/production CTAs. Original informational sections remain accessible. No generic duplicate question forms are added to the menu.

Organization account exposes requisites, staff/permissions, delivery recipients, invoices/documents and EDI. These screens use the source integration boundary and must receive verified server data. A guest/company information choice never changes legal person type or authorizes invoices by itself.
