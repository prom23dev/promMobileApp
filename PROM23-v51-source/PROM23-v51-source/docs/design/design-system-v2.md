# Design system V2 / implemented V8

Brand source: supplied layout and real company materials. Tokens: mobile/src/ui/tokens.ts → semantic theme.ts → component styles. Navy brand, white surfaces, orange action accent, green Split and violet Plus explain purpose. Keep the real logo, phone and contacts visible. Avoid ornamental gradients, fabricated status counters and performance-costly background effects.

Hero combines the real production image with a clear equipment proposition and two visible paths: catalog and procurement. Company evidence is shared by B2C/B2B;2008 is company founding, not the start of factory output. Real awards have exact names, without counting duplicate diploma/statuette entries. Export geography is sourced, historical metrics remain dated.

Typography: legible system native UI, hierarchy31/23/16/15/12 with explicit line heights; reusable button48px, visible focus, expandable text at larger accessibility sizes. The screen content max-width840 prevents stretched reading columns. Catalog2 columns collapse to1 under360px/large type; category shapes are semantic SVG pictograms. Product photos remain on products.

Functional decoration only: status color, selected tabs, evidence imagery, disclosure indicators. The initial buyer segment no longer creates a fictitious organization. Page and sheet back actions are preserved. Search stays search; the menu has its own button.

Verification: typecheck/unit suites, mobile-browser viewport and critical flows, followed by design critic screenshots. Subjective quality is not reported as a measured95% acceptance rate. See testing/regression-matrix.md for actual evidence.
