# B2C V2

B2C and B2B have equal product importance. Guests can browse, compare, favorite and prepare a cart without adding an organization. A company account requires verified membership rather than a visual toggle. Existing company draft is retained and can be sent into the file-first procurement form.

Home has BuyerBenefits with four interactive choices: Split, Plus via Pay, delivery and account. Conditions are readable before a purchase and link to the full source-derived information pages. Product cards and detail pages show the same backend price/old price/discount, purchase-benefit disclosures, delivery and contact access. No invented bonus balance, fixed cashback percentage, delivery date or warehouse stock.

The personalized shelves use viewed products and matching source categories; no hidden behavioral network tracking. Search history can be removed. Account explains orders, saved addresses and documents, includes WaitingList and comparison, and exposes company services with equal clarity.

Settlement selection is explicit, structured and disambiguated by official type/region/district. Free text is never persisted as a settlement. A region change invalidates offers while preserving the cart. Real recalculation depends on the connected source backend.
